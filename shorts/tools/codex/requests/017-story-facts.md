Review as a physics teacher, a fact-checker and a Shorts editor:

1. **Physics.** Is each scenario right?
   - Facing backward → you fall backward.
   - Sideways → you twist or spin.
   - Facing forward and running it out → the stop is spread over many steps.
   - The rough force numbers (about 3× body weight stopping in one step vs a fraction over about 1 s).

   Anything we oversimplify into a wrong statement? Is there a better, still-true way to say any of it?
2. **Facts.** Any claim that isn't supported by its sources, or that needs a caveat? The "hundreds die
   every year" line and the "10 km/h" framing especially.
3. **Safety and YouTube policy.** Could any beat read as a how-to or as glorifying the act? The forward
   run is the physics payoff, immediately undercut by the twist. Is that enough?
4. **Engagement.**
   - Hook strength.
   - Does the 48 s hold (the drone, speedometer, ghost, three falls, twist, stop)?
   - Is the payoff at about 65-70%?
   - Is the loop line clean?
   - Which beat would make people swipe away, and what's the fix?
5. **Top 3 changes** before we write the script. Under 600 words.
