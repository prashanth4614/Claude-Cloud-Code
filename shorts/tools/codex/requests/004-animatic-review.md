## My review (argue with it)
1. **Hook (S01)** is a question over a slow plane reveal. Zack opens on the consequence. Proposal: a
   0.6 s cold open of the eardrum bulging/Max wincing, then the plane.
2. **The POP payoff (S09b, 35.4 s) is weak**: a dolly-in on the same disc with spheres. Proposal: the
   eardrum snaps flat with a ring shockwave and a burst of orange spheres down the tube, a 2-frame
   flash, then a 0.6 s cut to Max's relief. Currently there's no Max reaction to the POP at all.
3. **The bulge and inward dent aren't readable** (S06, S11): we always look face-on at the disc. Add a
   side/profile angle (or the x-ray map) so the membrane's curvature is the subject.
4. **Spheres clutter the frame**: in S04/S06/S11 a near orange sphere covers a third of the frame and
   hides the eardrum. Fewer, smaller, kept off the camera path.
5. **Long shots**: S06 5.0 s, S08 5.0 s, S11 5.4 s, S03 4.7 s. Split each into 2 cuts on the beats.
6. **S12b** has Max yawning and chewing in a medium-wide shot, too small to read. Proposal: three
   quick CUs, one per word (swallow / yawn / chew), ~0.6 s each.
7. **S04m x-ray map** is static for 3.3 s and reads like a textbook diagram. Push in slowly and animate
   the spheres balancing across the eardrum.
8. **S05a** says "climbs" but the plane sits level and small. It should visibly climb (nose up, rising).
9. **S08** "cracks open": the tube opening is a dark hole you barely notice. It should visibly
   unpeel/open on "cracks" (30.9 s) with spheres rushing in.
10. **Captions** are full sentences in a small monospace box near the bottom, where the Shorts UI
    covers them. Proposal: bold sans, 2–3 words at a time, key word highlighted, at ~60% height.
11. Grey first/last frames in S03 and S04 (likely the camera passing through the membrane) and the
    blocky brown frame at the end of S02's push-through: check that these read as intentional
    transitions, not glitches.
12. The malleus reads as a pencil. It needs the real ossicle shape (BodyParts3D, CC-BY) for the
    "inside the ear" shots to feel credible.
