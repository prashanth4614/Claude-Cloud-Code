# G3 frame review: rough boards for the train step-off Short (look at the image; no code)

These are **blockout boards**: grey Workbench renders of a procedural Mumbai station and an EMU coach.
Max is in a neutral A-pose and is only placed and leaned (no posed limbs or faces yet). An amber capsule
stands in for the "inertia ghost"; amber arrows show motion and teal arrows show grip. Judge
composition, staging and readability, not surface quality.

Context:
- the shot list: `shorts/train_step_off/shots.yaml`;
- the story and physics kit: `shorts/train_step_off/story.md`;
- the locked script: `shorts/train_step_off/script.md`;
- the one-3D-world rule and transitions: `.claude/skills/shorts-craft/SKILL.md` §1-§3.

The train runs toward screen-right (+X) in every shot. Every fall leans that way, whichever way Max faces;
that's the physics we're showing.

Review each board (1-11):
1. Does it explain its line at phone size, in under a second?
2. Is screen direction consistent across the cuts (the train and the falls always moving the same way)?
3. Will the transition chain in shots.yaml work from these framings (match cuts, whips, rewinds)?
4. Safe zone: is anything important in the bottom 22% (captions and the Shorts UI)?
5. Which boards are weak or confusing, and what framing or staging fixes them?
6. Is anything missing that the story needs (e.g. a beat that shows the train is still moving)?

Rank the fixes, then give a verdict: is the shot design ready for key frames (G6), or does it need
another board pass? Under 600 words.
