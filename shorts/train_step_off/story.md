# Why you fall stepping off a slow train: G1 beat outline (draft for the owner)

Target ≈ 48 s (owner: up to ~50 s, tight). Rough words at 170 wpm ≈ 120-130. The feeling at each beat
comes first; the wording is only a guide (the script is G2).

| # | Time (s) | Beat | What we see (one 3D world, Mumbai local) | Viewer feels | Line (rough) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 | **Hook** (drone dive) | Aerial over the tracks and high-rises; a white-purple local snakes into the station; the camera dives along the coach to an open door: Max leaning out, eyeing the platform | "I've seen this a thousand times" | "Getting off a slow train looks easy. Physics disagrees." |
| 2 | 3-8 | **Stakes** (speedometer) | Whip into the motorman's cab: analogue dial, needle sliding 30 → 20 → 15; whip back: Max shifts his weight, platform sliding past | Tension: he's going to do it | "At ten kilometres an hour, the train still looks like it's crawling. That's more than twice your walking speed." |
| 3 | 8-14 | **Cause** (the ghost) | Slow motion: Max steps out; the instant his foot touches, an amber "inertia ghost" of him keeps gliding forward at train speed while his foot is stopped by friction (squeak) | "Oh, the body keeps going" | "Your feet hit the platform and stop. The rest of you is still doing ten." |
| 4 | 14-21 | **Wrong way 1: facing back** | Rewind; Max steps off facing backward; the ghost surges past behind him; he tips backward; freeze a hand's width before his head meets the concrete (thud on the freeze) | Wince | "Facing backward, your body falls the way you can't see. Head first." |
| 5 | 21-27 | **Wrong way 2: sideways** | Rewind; he steps straight out; feet stop, torso twists along the platform, legs scissor; freeze mid-spin | "Not that either" | "Step out sideways, and your legs can't follow. You spin." |
| 6 | 27-34 | **Payoff: facing forward** (≈ 65-70 %) | Rewind; facing the direction of travel; the ghost is ahead of him and he runs after it, three, four, five steps, the momentum arrow shrinking each step; he stays up | Relief, "aha" | "Face forward and run it out: you stop over many steps instead of one, so your legs can catch you." |
| 7 | 34-42 | **Twist: even then** | Same forward run, but a wet patch, a bag, the platform edge: his heel slips toward the gap between train and platform; freeze just before | Shock | "But one slip, one bag, one wet patch, and the gap is right there. In Mumbai, hundreds die every year falling from moving trains." |
| 8 | 42-48 | **Takeaway + loop** | Rewind all the way to the door; the dial needle drops to 0; the train stops; Max steps off calmly, looks at camera; the framing matches the opening door shot | Settled, shareable | "So the only safe speed to step off at is zero. Wait for the stop. Because getting off looks easy…" (loops into the hook) |

**Devices:** the inertia ghost (amber, translucent) and the momentum arrow attached in 3D; a rewind
between scenarios (reverse motion blur and a tape-rewind whoosh); the speedometer as the recurring
anchor (opening, and the final 0).

**Tone guard (YouTube policy):** no "how to". The forward run is the physics payoff, then immediately
undercut by the twist; the takeaway is "wait for the stop". Falls freeze before impact; no injury shown.
