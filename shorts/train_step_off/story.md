# Why you fall stepping off a slow train: G1 beat outline (rev 2, after Codex 017)

Target ≈ 44 s (owner: up to ~50 s, tight). Narration ≈ 110-118 words at 170 wpm, leaving room for the
"hear it" moments and freezes. The feeling at each beat comes first; the wording is only a guide (the
script is G2).

| # | Time (s) | Beat | What we see (one 3D world, Mumbai local) | Viewer feels | Line (rough) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-2.5 | **Hook** (fast drone dive) | A sub-second aerial dive along the local into the station, landing tight on Max's shoe at the open door edge, the platform blurring past below | Danger, right away | "Getting off a slow train looks easy. Physics disagrees." |
| 2 | 2.5-6 | **Stakes** (speedometer) | A whip into the cab: the needle sliding 20 → 15 → **10**; a whip back to Max shifting his weight | Tension | "At ten kilometres an hour it looks like a crawl. That's more than twice a comfortable walking pace." |
| 3 | 6-11 | **Cause** (the ghost) | Slow motion: his foot plants and the shoe squeaks as it grips; an amber "inertia ghost" keeps gliding along the platform at train speed while his lower body slows sharply | "Oh, the body keeps going" | "Your planted foot slows sharply. Your upper body doesn't. It keeps moving at train speed." |
| 4 | 11-17 | **Facing back** | A rewind snap; he steps off facing backward; the ghost runs ahead behind his back; he topples backward, the way he can't see; **freeze before impact** (no impact sound) | Wince | "Face backward, and you tip the way you can't see." |
| 5 | 17-21 | **Sideways** (compressed, no full rewind: a quick reverse snap) | He steps straight out; his body keeps going along the platform and throws him off balance; freeze | "Not that either" | "Step out sideways, and it throws you off balance." |
| 6 | 21-30 | **Payoff, with its limit inside it** (≈ 68-70 %) | Facing forward, a few quick steps, the momentum arrow shrinking each step… then a wobble: the arrow still large, his balance on a knife edge | "Aha… but uh-oh" | "Facing forward, a few quick steps can spread the slowdown. But they can't promise your balance." |
| 7 | 30-38 | **Twist** | One step lands on a wet patch; his heel slides toward the gap between train and platform; freeze just before | Shock | "One wet patch, one bag, one crowded step, and the gap is right there." |
| 8 | 38-44 | **Takeaway + loop** | A rewind all the way to the door; the dial needle reaches **0**; the train stops; Max steps off calmly; the framing matches the opening door shot | Settled | "So wait until the train has fully stopped. Because…" (loops into "getting off a slow train looks easy…") |

**Casualty context goes in the description, not the narration** (Codex 017: the figures mix doorway
falls with gap deaths). Description line: "Government Railway Police figures: 6,760 people died falling
from moving trains on Mumbai's suburban network between Jan 2014 and May 2025 (PTI). Railways urge
passengers never to board or leave a moving train."

**Devices:**
- The inertia ghost (amber, translucent), moving relative to the platform. Inertia isn't a force:
  nothing pushes Max.
- A momentum arrow attached in 3D.
- Rewind snaps.
- The speedometer: 10 at the start, 0 at the end.

**Tone guard (YouTube policy):**
- No imperative "how to".
- The forward run carries its own limit ("can't promise your balance").
- Every fall freezes before impact, with no impact sound and no injury.
- The takeaway is "wait until the train has fully stopped".

**Codex 017 changes applied:**
1. No fall is shown as certain. No "head first" and no "no step to save it"; sideways "throws you off
   balance" rather than "spin".
2. "Feet stop" became "the planted foot slows sharply". No force numbers on screen or in the
   narration.
3. Coaching language removed; the danger is inside the payoff.
4. The dial reaches 10 before contact; the opener and the sideways beat are compressed.
5. The casualty line moved to the description.
6. "The only safe speed is zero" became "Wait until the train has fully stopped".
