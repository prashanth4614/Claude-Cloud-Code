## Myths checked (so we don't repeat one)

- "You fall straight down" (Vikash_Toons Short): wrong; you pitch in the direction of motion.
- "Running alongside makes it safe" (several clips): partly; it reduces the force but doesn't remove the
  risk. We say so explicitly.
- "It's illegal under the Railways Act": no specific section; avoid.
